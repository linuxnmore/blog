### Welcome to Linux n’ More!

#### C.T.’s Story
Linux n’ More is a blog operated by C.T. Peck, a computer enthusiast out of Dallas, Texas, who holds a fascination with open source technologies. Mr. Peck has been programming since the age of 14, when he first picked up rudimentary Python. He went on to learn the ins and outs of Java at 16, further carrying on his love for computing. By the age of 18, Mr. Peck began to face the broader world, and his interest had to take a backseat for a time. However, it returned with a fiery passion within 2 years, upon which time he truly discovered his first love: Linux. 

Immediately switching his computer’s operating system to Fedora upon receipt of the computer, he began to tinker and play, skills he had learned returning back to him in a way he had never seen them. Linux wasn’t just an operating system; Linux was a powerful tool that enabled the user to take into their own hands what others obscured. Eventually becoming more obsessed with lower-lever control, he would switch to Arch Linux, when he began to learn about ricing and even more advanced user controls. 

The latest Linux obsession was NixOS, a fine little OS with some cool abilities that he can’t wait to discuss. Anything and everything open source will be opined upon on this blog and its conjoined YouTube channel.

Mr. Peck thanks you all from the bottom of his heart for your readership.

---
#### What You’ll Find
On this blog, you’ll find guides, commentary, news, and other miscellaneous content related to anything FOSS. Mr. Peck is dedicated to promoting the usage of FOSS, hoping this blog will serve as yet another cry into the cacophony of public discourse, hoping to direct the crowd towards it. From cell phone apps to PC operating systems, Mr. Peck intends to cover it all.
